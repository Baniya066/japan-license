Common questions

What is free, and what does full access unlock?

Set 1 (50 questions) is free, and the first 10 questions of Sets 2 and 3 are free. One payment unlocks all 500 questions: every practice set, test sets 6 to 10, the mock exams and the 25 picture scenes. It is a one-time payment, not a subscription.

I paid, but the app is still locked. Or: I have a new phone.

Open the app, tap “See unlock options”, then tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that you used to pay. A purchase made on iPhone does not move to Android, or the other way.

My payment says it is waiting for approval.

This can happen with Ask to Buy or with some payment methods. Full access turns on by itself when the payment is approved.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested from them. Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com · Google Play: Google Play refund help

I found a mistake in a question.

Email us the set number and the question text (a screenshot is best). We check every report and fix the app in an update.

How do I change the help language or reset my progress?

Help language: on the home screen or in Settings. Reset: My progress → Reset all progress. This deletes your answers and results from your phone.

Is this the official exam?

No. Japan License is an independent study app. It is not connected to the police or to any driver’s license center. Rules can change, so please also check the official information from your prefecture’s license center.

Privacy policyगोपनीयता नीतिগোপনীয়তা নীতি

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Japan License app (“the app”) uses. The app is made by Pathway Learn (“we”).

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.

1. Information stored only on your phone

Your answers, mistakes, exam results, your place in a practice set and your help language are saved on your device so the app can show your progress. This information is not sent to us. You can delete it with My progress → Reset all progress, or by deleting the app.

2. Purchases

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. To confirm your purchase and let you restore it, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID (not your name or email), your purchase and transaction details, and basic device and app information such as the operating system, app version and store country. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: www.revenuecat.com/privacy

3. Listen feature

Reading a question aloud uses your phone’s built-in text-to-speech. The app does not send that text to us.

4. What we do not do

We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or camera. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.

5. Children

The app is a study tool for adults preparing for a driver’s license. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices and requests

We do not keep your progress on our servers, so we cannot see or recover it. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, or any other privacy question, email japanlicense.support@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

আমরা আপনার নাম, ইমেল, অবস্থান, পরিচিতি, ছবি বা ক্যামেরা চাই না। আমরা বিজ্ঞাপন দেখাই না, অ্যানালিটিক্স বা ট্র্যাকিং টুল ব্যবহার করি না, এবং বিজ্ঞাপনের জন্য আপনার তথ্য বিক্রি বা শেয়ার করি না।

5. শিশু

অ্যাপটি ড্রাইভিং লাইসেন্সের প্রস্তুতি নেওয়া প্রাপ্তবয়স্কদের জন্য একটি পড়াশোনার টুল। এটি ১৩ বছরের কম বয়সী শিশুদের জন্য নয়, এবং আমরা জেনেশুনে শিশুদের ব্যক্তিগত তথ্য সংগ্রহ করি না।

6. আপনার পছন্দ ও অনুরোধ

আমরা আপনার অগ্রগতি আমাদের সার্ভারে রাখি না, তাই আমরা তা দেখতে বা ফিরিয়ে আনতে পারি না। RevenueCat-এর কাছে থাকা কেনার তথ্য বা গোপনীয়তা নিয়ে অন্য যেকোনো প্রশ্নের জন্য japanlicense.support@gmail.com ঠিকানায় ইমেল করুন।
